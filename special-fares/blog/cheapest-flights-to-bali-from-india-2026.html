The Three Routes from India to Bali Right Now

There's no single direct non-stop from most of India to Bali — but the three best options differ by baggage, layover length, and in-flight experience:

  • Air India (DEL-DPS direct) — Launched this year, 11 hours non-stop. 25 KG + 7 KG baggage. ₹52,999-₹54,999 for Jan-Feb 2027. Best for travellers who hate connections.
  • Malaysia Airlines (DEL/ATQ-KUL-DPS) — One connection in Kuala Lumpur. 30 KG + 7 KG baggage (the most generous of the three). Round trips from ₹48,999-₹52,999.
  • Singapore Airlines (DEL-SIN-DPS) — The premium cabin experience even in economy. 25 KG + 7 KG. Fixed round-trip at ₹52,999 across Jan-Mar 2027.

What Decides a Fare This Low?

1. Blocked Inventory, Not Live GDS

These fares aren't the ones Skyscanner or Google Flights show. They come from seats the airline pre-sells in bulk to consolidators and tour operators. Specialist travel agents in India (like Voyage-Ed) hold these seats and can confirm within 30 minutes. Public portals never surface them.

2. Non-Refundable, Non-Changeable

This is the trade-off. The ₹50,999 fare is locked — once issued, the ticket can't be cancelled or changed. For travellers with firm dates (honeymoon, planned holiday, visa-stamped travel), this is a non-issue. For flexible travellers, pay ₹8,000-₹12,000 more for a refundable ticket.

3. 4-Week Advance Minimum

These fares require booking at least 25-30 days before departure. The closer you get to travel date, the less likely blocked inventory remains open. Last-minute buyers pay full published fares.

The Best Time to Visit Bali from India

The flight pricing and the weather pricing move in opposite directions in Bali — which is why savvy buyers target the shoulder months:

  • Peak season (Jul-Aug, Dec 20 - Jan 5) — Highest flight fares + heaviest crowds + room rates up 40-60%. Avoid unless you have no choice.
  • Sweet spot (Feb-Mar + Apr-May) — Dry season, light crowds, flights at their lowest. This is where our ₹48,999-₹52,999 blocked fares are concentrated.
  • Monsoon (Nov-early Feb) — Rain in short afternoon bursts, hotels are cheapest, flights reasonable.

Hotel & Villa Pricing Context

Once you've locked the flight, hotel budgets in Bali run three clean tiers:

  • ₹3,500-₹6,000 per night — Boutique hotels in Seminyak, Ubud, or Canggu (4★ equivalent, breakfast included, infinity pool).
  • ₹8,000-₹14,000 per night — Private villas with pool in Seminyak, Nusa Dua, or Uluwatu. The honeymoon tier.
  • ₹22,000+ per night — Cliff-top luxury (Alila Uluwatu, Four Seasons Jimbaran). Save these for anniversaries.

Flight + 7 nights + VOA + airport transfers for two people from Delhi sits around ₹1.6-₹2.2 lakh total in the Jan-March window using blocked fares plus boutique accommodation.

Booking Checklist Before Departure

  • Passport with at least 6 months validity beyond travel date
  • Visa on Arrival paid in USD or IDR cash at Denpasar (approx ₹3,000 for 30 days)
  • Return ticket confirmed and printed (occasionally checked)
  • Proof of accommodation booking
  • Travel insurance covering Southeast Asia (we include this in our blocked-fare packages)
  • Two passport photos as backup

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the cheapest Delhi-Bali round-trip fare right now?
The cheapest Delhi-Bali round trip in our blocked inventory is ₹50,999 with Malaysia Airlines (via Kuala Lumpur, 30 KG baggage), valid for 16-23 January 2027 travel. The same sector runs ₹49,000-₹53,000 across Jan-Mar 2027 depending on the week.
Is there a direct flight from Amritsar to Bali?
There's no non-stop ATQ-DPS route yet. The best routing is Amritsar → Kuala Lumpur → Bali on Malaysia Airlines (MH 119 + MH 715). Our blocked round-trip fare starts at ₹49,999 for Feb-Mar 2027.
Which airline is best for Delhi-Bali from India?
For luggage and connection time, Malaysia Airlines (30 KG + 7 KG, via KUL) and Singapore Airlines (25 KG + 7 KG, via SIN) are the two most comfortable options. Air India now operates a direct Delhi-Bali, saving 5-6 hours of connection time with 25 KG baggage.
Do Indians need a visa for Bali?
Visa on Arrival is available for Indians at Denpasar Airport. Cost is approximately IDR 500,000 (~₹3,000) for 30 days. Carry two recent passport-size photos and proof of return ticket.
What's the best month to visit Bali from India?
February and March 2027 are the sweet spot right now — dry season, lower crowds, and our deepest blocked inventory (fares start ₹48,999 for the Amritsar return). Avoid 20 Dec - 5 Jan (peak Indian holiday overlap) when fares jump 40%.
